Live: M=8 N≈12576 K=4096, grid 8x99x1, 456 us, kda.py:327 ColumnParallel F.linear.
Layout: F.linear TN (kernel `tn_align8`), weight (N,K) bf16, no extra transpose.
Isolated: 440–454 us, 227–234 GB/s (83–86% of 273). DRAM roof 377 us.
Ceiling if at roof: (456-377)/456 * 10.75% ≈ **1.9% CUDA** ≈ **< +2.5% soak keep bar**.
Workspace did not select a faster algo. Fusion/qkv split/TP/approximation forbidden.

**Decision: SKIP. No code/env change.**

## Work 3 — shared_experts.gate_up_proj (A, 6.03% CUDA)

Live: 351 launches, avg 209 us, grid 8x16x1, N_est=2048, 16x16_128x2.
Config: n_shared_experts=1, moe_intermediate=2048, TP=2 fused N=2048, bf16.
Isolated SAME kernel (128x2):
  hot (L2 reuse)  34 us  ~500 GB/s  — not live (42 distinct 16.8 MB weights, L2=24 MB)
  cold (42 W)     78 us  ~216 GB/s  (79% of DRAM roof 61 us)
Live 209 us vs isolated-cold 78 us is concurrent traffic (Marlin ~42% CUDA), not a
wrong algo. Closing that gap requires parking Marlin (forbidden) or a new kernel
that uses less DRAM under contention — not available without _C rebuild (forbidden).
Realistic ceiling vs DRAM roof: 6.03*(1-61/209) ≈ 4.3% CUDA ≈ +1.5 tok/s, below keep bar.
Workspace :4096:8 only helps L2-hot isolated (26→21 us), not live.

**Decision: SKIP. Kernel name 128x2 is not a reason to swap.**

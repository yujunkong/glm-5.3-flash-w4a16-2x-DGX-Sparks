144 CTAs = 1 per local expert (288/TP2). topk=8, M=8 → most CTAs idle.
Occupancy: 3 blocks/SM, 384 threads/SM on 48 SMs.
Correction (probe_marlin_bw_unique.py): moe_align emits **one block per unique expert only** (~49–57), not 144 empty CTAs. Live grid 144 is persistent CTAs (`sms * blocks_per_sm`), not expert count.

w1 W4 = 4.19 MB/expert. 49 uniq × 4.19 = 205 MB / live 819 µs ≈ **251 GB/s (92% of 273)**.
w2 = 2.10 MB/expert. 54 uniq × 2.10 / 468 µs ≈ **242 GB/s (89%)**.

Marlin is already LPDDR-bound. Launch knobs ±2% matches that. Not 98 GB/s; that used the wrong grid meaning.

## Rewrite that was not done

A faster-than-DRAM kernel is not available: w1/w2 are already ~90% of 273 GB/s. Remaining ~8–12% on the Marlin op is ~3–5% CUDA ≈ +1 tok/s, below keep bar. Do not write a new W4 kernel for this.
